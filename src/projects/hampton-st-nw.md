## The project

A modern side-by-side duplex in Home Park, minutes from Georgia Tech and Midtown. Each unit stacks three levels: a bedroom suite and covered carport at street level, an open kitchen and living floor with a second suite and terrace, and two more suites with a top-floor terrace above.

## Built to hold

Every bedroom has its own full bath, which makes the layout work for families, roommates or student tenants. The plan is to complete construction and hold both units as long-term rentals.

## Details

- About 1,570 heated sq ft per unit, 3,140 total
- Flat-roof modern exterior with wood-slat accents and floor-to-ceiling glass
- Separate entries and driveways for each unit
