## Now available

220 Maple Place is a brand-new stacked duplex in Vine City, finished in 2026. Each unit is a 2-bedroom, 2-bath home of roughly 610 square feet with its own kitchen, so every resident gets a private bath and shares the kitchen with one roommate.

- Open kitchens with quartz islands and LVP flooring
- Private balconies
- Unfinished full basement (about 590 sf) with room to add value later

## Built for owners and investors

Live in one unit and let the other help pay the mortgage, or lease by the room to students from Morehouse, Spelman, Clark Atlanta and Morehouse School of Medicine. Projected by-the-room rent is about $1,200 per bedroom per month. Figures are estimates, not guarantees.

## Location

About 0.3 miles to Vine City MARTA (Blue/Green lines), walking distance to Mercedes-Benz Stadium, and minutes from the Atlanta University Center.
