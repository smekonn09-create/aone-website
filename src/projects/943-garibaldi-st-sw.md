## The project

A modern three-story home in Pittsburgh, close to the BeltLine's Westside Trail and minutes from downtown. Clean white siding, warm wood soffits and black-framed glass give it a crisp, high-end look.

## Layout

- **Main level:** open kitchen with island, dining and living areas, foyer and attached garage
- **Second level:** primary suite with wet-room bath and walk-in closet, a fourth bedroom with its own bath, laundry and a lounge opening to a balcony
- **Top level:** two bedrooms with a shared bath, a loft, and a terrace bar that opens to a covered rooftop terrace
